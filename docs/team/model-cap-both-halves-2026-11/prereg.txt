THE POST-CALIBRATION STEP, for every stored week W, in the pipeline's order
(hub rows, then the cap; the historical ceiling is off, as registered and
as submitted):
- rows = rows_from_quantiles(q, fips, W), whole admissions, every state.
- obs(fips): the week's vintage (vintage_path(W)), rows dated on or before
  W, finite values, oldest first, the last 15, as the pipeline builds it.
- the level, three variants: R3 = recent_level(obs) (the mean of the last
  three; production); R1 = the last finite value of obs; RX = max(R3, R1).
  A location whose level is 0, NaN or missing is left alone in every arm.
- the upper limit L = n x level, for n in {10, 15}.
- the location exponent k = min over horizons of exponent_needed(block_h,
  L), the blocks taken from rows (as cap_rows takes them); k = 1 leaves the
  location alone in every mode.
- per block (location, horizon), median m, levels q:
  S  (one-sided; production): cap_rows(rows, L, mode="shrink"), the
     repository's own call; the median and below untouched.
  M  (the mirror): q' = m (q/m)^k for every level with q > 0, both halves
     (q = 0 stays 0); the upper half equals S's before rounding.
  B  (a separate lower-half exponent): the upper half as S; below the
     median q' = m (q/m)^kL with kL = min(1, min over horizons h of
     k x ln(q97.5_h / m_h) / ln(m_h / q2.5_h)), the horizons taken where
     q2.5_h > 0 and m_h > q2.5_h and q97.5_h > m_h, from the rows before
     the cap; kL = 1 when k = 1. In words: once the cap acts, the lower half
     is narrowed just enough that at every horizon the 95% band is no
     wider below the median than above it on the log scale.
  LN (a lower narrowing): the upper half as S; below the median q' =
     m (q/m)^0.7 in every block with m > 0 (the narrowing's exponent,
     oracle.narrow 0.7, applied to the lower half only; independent of
     the cap and the level).
  Then whole_block(capped, block) for every mode: the moved levels in
  whole admissions (half to even), the block monotone.

DECISION RULE (this lane's recommendation to Ely; Ely and the PI choose n,
10x having been their joint choice for the peers' widths, so the rule picks
a mode and a level at each n and does not pick n):
At each n, an arm X n-V other than S n-R3 is RECOMMENDED over S n-R3 when
  (a) X n-V - S n-R3 pooled <= 0, the bootstrap interval's upper end at
      most +0.002;
  (b) no season is worse than S n-R3 by more than 0.005;
  (c) EARLY: X n-V - S n-R3 <= +0.003;
  (d) the log scale, pooled: X n-V - S n-R3 <= +0.002;
  (e) 95% coverage pooled at least 0.90 and 50% at least 0.40; on EARLY
      95% at least 0.85;
  (f) the code check finds no defect.
Among the arms that pass, the lowest pooled relWIS; within 0.001 of it,
the simpler, in this order: level R3, RX, R1; mode M, B, LN. If none
passes, S n-R3 stays the recommendation and the failing tests are named.
The Groundhog half alone does not move the Oracle; a mode for both members
waits for Part 2 unless Ely decides otherwise.
