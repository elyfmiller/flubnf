AMENDMENT 1 TO THE TAIL CAP ON BOTH HALVES (prereg 67262c94): A LOWER HALF
BALANCED IN ADMISSIONS (BN), THE GROUNDHOG HALF
Model lane (Ely's Windows PC), written 2026-11-11 after Part 1's scores
(86f194f0) and before any score of the arm below.

WHY, AND WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN. Ely, 2026-11-11, on reading Part 1: after a
jump like Delaware's, the cap squeezes the top while the bottom keeps its
width, so the bottom half can be wider than the capped top. That looks
wrong and likely costs WIS; it should be looked at before the 2026-11-11
run. Part 1 (report.txt) tested three lower-half rules, and all of them
narrowed the lower half too far for the Groundhog: 95% coverage fell (10x
R3: S 0.904, M 0.884, B 0.876, LN 0.867), most in October 2023, where the
truth already fell below the band (no cap: 8.2% below the 2.5% quantile).
B balanced the halves on the log scale, which pulls the 2.5% quantile up
to m^2 / q97.5. The rule below balances them in admissions, which pulls it
only to 2m - q97.5, so it acts only where the band is wider below the
median than above it in the forecast's own units. This arm is chosen
knowing Part 1's results, so it is held to Part 1's rule and to a second
test against the one-sided shrink at the same level.

THE ARM. Data, code, the post-calibration step, the levels, the limits and
the location exponent k are Part 1's. Per location:
  BN (balanced in admissions): the upper half as S (cap_rows' shrink with
  k). Below the median, q' = m (q/m)^kN for every level with q > 0, where
  kN = min(1, min over horizons h of e_h), and e_h is computed on the rows
  before the cap, with q97.5'_h = m_h (q97.5_h / m_h)^k (the capped top,
  unrounded):
    t_h = 2 m_h - q97.5'_h (the 2.5% quantile that makes the band as wide
    below the median as above it);
    e_h = ln(m_h / t_h) / ln(m_h / q2.5_h) where m_h > 0, q2.5_h > 0 and
    q2.5_h < t_h < m_h; otherwise e_h = 1.
  kN = 1 when k = 1. A horizon whose 2.5% quantile is 0 is left alone
  (counted and reported). Then whole_block, as for every arm.
Arms: BN10-R3, BN10-RX, BN15-R3, BN15-RX. They are scored on the same
17,116 cells, with Part 1's subsets, coverage, tails and h0 lean.

CODE CHECK, in addition to Part 1's (i) to (iii) and (v): (vi) where kN < 1,
at every horizon whose e_h was computed, the moved 2.5% quantile is at
least 2m - q97.5 of the capped rows, within one admission; (vii) BN's
levels above the median are identical to S's at the same n and V.

PREDICTIONS, written now:
  H1 BN changes fewer state blocks below the median than B at every n and
     V.
  H2 BN n-V - S n-V pooled in [-0.004, +0.001] for each of the four arms.
  H3 95% coverage: BN n-V at most 0.010 below S n-V, for each arm.
  H4 Sep-Oct (2023-24): BN costs less than B against S at 10x R3 (BN10-R3
     - S10-R3 < B10-R3 - S10-R3).
  H5 On EARLY, BN10-R3 - S10-R3 <= 0.

DECISION RULE. BN n-V is RECOMMENDED over S n-V, the shrink alone at the
same cap and level, when
  (1) it passes Part 1's tests (a) to (f) against S n-R3; and
  (2) against S n-V: the pooled difference <= 0 with the bootstrap
      interval's upper end at most +0.002; no season worse by more than
      0.003; 95% coverage pooled at most 0.010 lower.
Otherwise not, with the failing tests named. The Groundhog half alone
does not move the Oracle. Nothing ships without Ely.
