{% call tips.heading("How it works", "hm-how", rich=True, tip_label="how it works") %} Weekly probabilistic forecasts of influenza hospital admissions for all 52 US jurisdictions in the CDC FluSight format, from two models: the Oracle SIHRS, a mechanistic model fitted each week, and the Groundhog, an empirical calendar model. One weekly data pull feeds the Oracle SIHRS (the particle-filter fit, its forecast growth then blended with donor growth from past seasons at the same calendar week) and the Groundhog; each model's quantiles are written as its own FluSight CSV, nothing is blended between the two, and the weekly report shows both. {% endcall %} {# labels audited to fit their boxes at A+ (115%); aria-label, not a title element (its tooltip pops over the drawing) #}
