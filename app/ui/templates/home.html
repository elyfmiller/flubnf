Forecast the flu season, mechanistically.

FluBNF produces weekly probabilistic forecasts of influenza hospital admissions for all 52 US jurisdictions, in the CDC FluSight submission format and with a shareable weekly report. Two models are submitted each week: the Oracle SIHRS, a mechanistic transmission model fitted to each week's data whose forecast growth is then blended with past seasons' growth at the same calendar week, and the Groundhog, an empirical calendar model with banked donors.
