Influenza forecasting, operational and verifiable.

FluBNF produces weekly probabilistic forecasts of influenza hospital admissions for all 52 US jurisdictions, packaged in the CDC FluSight submission format together with a shareable weekly report. Runs, blending, scoring, and packaging are managed from this console.

How it works

A SIHRS compartmental model (Susceptible, Infected, Hospitalized, Recovered, with waning immunity) is written in BNGL and fitted with PyBNF's sequential particle filter on the bngsim engine. Each week, 10,000 candidate epidemics per state are reweighted by the newest admissions data; their spread is the forecast uncertainty. The full method, including a compartment diagram, is documented on the Methods page.

Two models, one forecast

The mechanistic model is paired with a calendar analogue, a purely empirical model of past seasons. Their errors disagree in useful ways, so the blended ensemble beats either member alone. Weights are frozen before the season from held-out years; nothing is tuned on the season being forecast.

Measured performance

In a three-season real-time retrospective (2023-24 through 2025-26), the ensemble beat the official CDC FluSight baseline in every season, with a pooled relative weighted interval score of 0.704. Scored against the real 2024-25 field, it would have placed 4th of the 40 participating teams. Every evaluation uses only the data available on each forecast date.

Weekly workflow

1. Data: confirm the feed is current

2. Forecast: select a date and run the models

3. Output: collect submission files and the report

A full past season can be replayed on the Retrospective tab.
