Forecast the flu season, mechanistically.

FluBNF produces weekly probabilistic forecasts of influenza hospital admissions for all 52 US jurisdictions, in the CDC FluSight submission format and with a shareable weekly report. A mechanistic transmission model is fitted to each week's data and blended with an empirical companion.
