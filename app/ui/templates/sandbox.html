A model is three files in sandbox/models/<name>/: model.bngl (its simulate action must name a suffix), data.exp (a time column and one count column), and priors.conf (one *_var line per free parameter, plus pf_cumulative_observable naming the model output whose weekly increment is the expected count, scale included). Runs here reach nothing else in the console.
